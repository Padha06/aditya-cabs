Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to all bookings made on shivrudrataxi.com

1. Cancellation by the Customer

  • Free cancellation up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time — 100% of any advance paid is refunded.
  • Cancellation within 2 hours of the scheduled pickup time — a nominal administrative fee of ₹300 is deducted; the balance advance is refunded.
  • No-show (customer not available at the pickup point after the driver waits) — the advance is non-refundable.

2. Refund of Advance Payment

  • Where a refund is due, it is credited back to the original payment method.
  • Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days (bank/UPI settlement timelines may vary).
  • For Cash on Drop bookings there is no online payment, so no refund applies; nothing is charged in advance.

3. Cancellation or Failure by Us

  • If we cannot provide a vehicle for a confirmed booking, 100% of any advance is refunded, promptly and in full.
  • If a trip is terminated mid-way for reasons attributable to us, the applicable fare is adjusted fairly and any excess advance is refunded.

4. Failed or Duplicate Payments

  • If an amount is debited but the booking is not confirmed, the amount is either auto-reversed by the bank/gateway or refunded on request.
  • Duplicate charges for the same booking are refunded in full after verification.
