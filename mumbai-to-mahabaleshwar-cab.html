About the Mumbai to Mahabaleshwar route

  • 230 kmDistance
  • 4h 45mDuration
  • ₹3,999Sedan from
  • ₹4,799SUV from

The Mumbai to Mahabaleshwar one-way cab covers about 230 km via the expressway and Wai and usually takes 4h 45m door to door. The fare is fixed before you travel: Sedan ₹3,999, SUV ₹4,799, toll and parking included. It is one way, so there is no return fare to pay.

Doorstep pickup in Mumbai covers Bandra, Andheri, Powai, Dadar, CSMT and both airport terminals; in Mahabaleshwar we drop you at the market, Venna Lake road and the Panchgani turn-off. One car does the whole trip, which makes it the practical option for station transfers, hotel check-ins, airport runs and family luggage.

Mahabaleshwar holds Venna Lake, Arthur's Seat, the strawberry farms and the Panchgani turn-off above the Krishna valley. Travellers book this cab for temple visits, hill weekends, business meetings and long-distance family travel without changing vehicles on the way.

When to travel: from Mumbai, start after the morning peak; weekend evenings are busiest. From Mahabaleshwar, leave in the morning to reach before the lake crowds.
