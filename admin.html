Welcome 👋

This is your control panel. Everything you save here updates the booking website straight away. Use the tabs above to move around.

What you can do here

  • Bookings — see today's, this week's and this month's bookings.
  • Routes & Fares — add a new route and set its price.
  • Cars — change a car's photo, name or price.
  • Trip Types — round-trip rates, local packages and shared-cab prices.
  • Settings — your phone number and the cities you serve.

Bookings

Latest bookings, newest first.

Routes & Fares

Each route runs both ways at the same price. Type the price for a Sedan and the price for an SUV. Prices for the other car types are worked out automatically (you can change that under “Cars”).

Cars

These are the car types customers choose. To change a photo, paste an image link (right-click any image online → “Copy image address”). “Priced from” and “Price factor” control how each car is priced — a factor of 1 means “same as its base price”.

Other trip types

Prices for round trips, local (hourly) rental and shared cabs.

Round trip

A round trip is priced per kilometre. We estimate the total from the kilometres below.

Local rental packages

Fixed prices for a few hours of city use.

Shared cab

Fixed price per seat. A maximum of 3 passengers share the car.

Settings

Your brand name and the phone number customers call and message.

Brand & contact

Site images

Change your logo, homepage photos and tab icon. Paste a direct image link (ends in .jpg, .png or .webp). The preview updates as you type.

Cities

Add each city or town you serve. These appear in the pickup/drop choices on a route.
