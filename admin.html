Welcome 👋

This is your control panel. Everything you save here updates the booking website straight away. Use the tabs above to move around.

What you can do here

  • Bookings — see today's, this week's and this month's bookings.
  • Routes & Fares — add a new route and set its price.
  • Cars — change a car's photo, name or price.
  • Trip Types — round-trip rates, local packages and shared-cab prices.
  • Settings — your phone number and the cities you serve.
