About the Ahilyanagar to Mumbai route

  • 255 kmDistance
  • 5h 30mDuration
  • ₹3,999Sedan from
  • ₹4,799SUV from

The Ahilyanagar to Mumbai one-way cab covers about 255 km via Nashik towards Mumbai and usually takes 5h 30m door to door. The fare is fixed before you travel: Sedan ₹3,999, SUV ₹4,799, toll and parking included. It is one way, so there is no return fare to pay.

Doorstep pickup in Ahilyanagar covers the city centre, the railway station and the Samruddhi interchange; in Mumbai we drop you at Bandra, Andheri, Powai, Dadar, CSMT and both airport terminals. One car does the whole trip, which makes it the practical option for station transfers, hotel check-ins, airport runs and family luggage.

Mumbai runs on Marine Drive, both airport terminals, the Bandra-Andheri business belt and the coastal road, with pickups possible at any hour. Travellers book this cab for temple visits, hill weekends, business meetings and long-distance family travel without changing vehicles on the way.

When to travel: from Ahilyanagar, any time of day; the junction is quick to cross. From Mumbai, start after the morning peak; weekend evenings are busiest.
