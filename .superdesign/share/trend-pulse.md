# trend-pulse - 2026-09-28

Lane: mobile booking / one-tap conversion (shares intent with our book + share loops).

Intent-serving trends (applied only where they fit):
1. **Kill form friction, don't decorate it** - Baymard/Shopify 2026: ~8 fields max, prefill everything, guest-first. We already prefill from profile; keep profile optional, never gate booking behind it.
2. **Thumb-zone primaries** - ootell/Baymard 2026: primary action in bottom half, one-handed. Our bottom bar + full-width submit buttons conform; keep CTAs out of top corners on tall phones.
3. **Wallets over typing** - 2026 checkout moves to Apple/Google Pay; our analogue is WhatsApp handoff + UPI-after-trip (no card typing anywhere). Keep it that way; the coming advance-payment step should offer UPI intent, not card forms.
4. **Speed is the feature** - 53% abandon past 3s on mobile. Static pages + width-matched images; no new blocking assets.

Rejected for this surface: BNPL/installments (irrelevant to taxi fares), app-only patterns (we are mobile web), AI chat booking (trust + language risk for this audience).
