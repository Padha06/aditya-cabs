About the Kolhapur to Pune route

  • 230 kmDistance
  • 5hDuration
  • ₹3,499Sedan from
  • ₹4,299SUV from

The Kolhapur to Pune one-way cab covers about 230 km via Karad and Satara and usually takes 5h door to door. The fare is fixed before you travel: Sedan ₹3,499, SUV ₹4,299, toll and parking included. It is one way, so there is no return fare to pay.

Doorstep pickup in Kolhapur covers the railway station, Rajarampuri, Mahadwar Road and the bus stand; in Pune we drop you at Kothrud, Baner, Hinjewadi, Viman Nagar, the railway station and Pune airport. One car does the whole trip, which makes it the practical option for station transfers, hotel check-ins, airport runs and family luggage.

Pune mixes the old-city wadas and Koregaon Park cafes with IT hubs in Hinjewadi and Kharadi, and it is the usual starting point for ghat getaways. Travellers book this cab for temple visits, hill weekends, business meetings and long-distance family travel without changing vehicles on the way.

When to travel: from Kolhapur, travel after breakfast for an easy ghat crossing. From Pune, depart before 8 am to clear the city rush.
