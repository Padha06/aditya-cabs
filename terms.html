Terms & Conditions & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Booking & Fare Guarantee

Shivrudra Taxi (shivrudrataxi.com) provides 24x7 one-way, round-trip, local hourly rental, and car-sharing taxi services throughout Maharashtra. For one-way fixed routes, the quoted fare includes state road tolls, parking fees, driver allowance, and doorstep pickup. Strictly no return fare is charged on one-way trips.

2. Outstation Round-Trip Fare Rules & Minimum Daily KM Limits

For outstation round-trip bookings, billing is determined on a per-day minimum kilometer basis plus extra km charges as follows:

  • Minimum Daily KM (1-Day Return Trip): For single-day return trips, a minimum of 300 km is charged per calendar day, even if the total distance driven is less than 300 km.
  • Multi-Day Trip Limit (2 Days or More): For multi-day round trips, the minimum chargeable distance is calculated as 300 km per calendar day (e.g. 2-day trip = minimum 600 km, 3-day trip = minimum 900 km).
  • Extra KM Charges (Beyond Daily Quota): Once the daily cumulative kilometer limit is crossed, extra distance is billed at the vehicle's standard fixed rate:
    • Sedan (Dzire / Aura / Etios): ₹12 / km
    • SUV Ertiga (6 Seater): ₹14 / km
    • Kia Carens (6–7 Seater): ₹17 / km
    • Innova Crysta (7 Seater VIP): ₹20 / km
    • Innova Hycross (Hybrid Luxury): ₹22 / km
  • Tolls, Parking & Entry Tax on Round Trips: Round trip fares never include tolls, parking or state entry tax. State tolls, expressway fees, parking and entry tax are always extra and payable on actuals.

3. Payment Modes & Advance Policy

  • Pay ₹500 Advance & Rest to Driver (Recommended): A nominal deposit of ₹500 reserves your vehicle and confirms driver allocation. Remaining fare is payable directly to driver at drop.
  • Pay Full Online: 100% advance via UPI / GPay / NetBanking / Cards via secure Cashfree PG checkout.
  • Pay After Drop / Cash on Cab: 100% fare payable directly to driver on arrival.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Free cancellation up to 2 hours before scheduled pickup time with 100% advance refund. For cancellations less than 2 hours before pickup, a nominal administrative fee of ₹300 applies. Refunds are credited back to the original payment source within 5–7 business days.

5. 24x7 Urgent Dispatch & Fleet

With 2,000+ drivers across all 36 Maharashtra districts, urgent bookings are dispatched within 15–30 minutes.
