Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you request a cab booking through Shivrudra Taxi (shivrudrataxi.com), we collect essential booking information including your name, contact phone number (WhatsApp), travel date, pickup location, and destination city. This information is collected solely to arrange vehicle dispatch, assign verified commercial drivers, and communicate trip status.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly for confirming and dispatching your requested cab across Maharashtra, sending driver details before your journey, customer support, and trip receipt generation.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your pickup address and contact number are only shared with the assigned verified driver for the specific duration of your scheduled trip.

4. Payment Security

Online advance payments are handled securely via authorized RBI gateways. Balance payments at drop are made directly to the driver via cash or UPI.

For any queries, reach our 24x7 central office at +91 86059 53737.
